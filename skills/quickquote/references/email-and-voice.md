# Email draft — tone and template

Create this as a **draft** with `mcp__Gmail__create_draft`. Never send it. Blaine reviews it in Gmail and sends it himself — that approval step is the whole point.

## Tone

Blaine's voice with a prospect who reached out for a quote: warm, low-pressure, competent. He's the specialist doing them a quick favor, not a salesman chasing. Short paragraphs. No hype, no fear-mongering, no fake urgency. Sound like a real person who knows this cold and is easy to talk to.

## Template

**To:** the prospect's email.

**Subject:** `Your long-term care options, [First Name]` — adjust the product word if it's a short-term/annuity-only teaser.

**Body** (adapt naturally; don't sound like a form letter):

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thanks for reaching out. I put together a quick overview of the options you'd qualify for so you can see the shape of it — the products, the range of coverage, and roughly what the premiums look like.
>
> [One sentence naming what's in it, e.g. "It covers a traditional long-term care option and a hybrid that keeps a death benefit if you never need care."]
>
> Here's your overview: [Gamma link]
>
> These are ballpark ranges to give you a feel for it. Your exact numbers depend on a few specifics, and I can pin those down with you in about 15 minutes — you can grab a time that works here: [scheduling link]
>
> Happy to answer anything in the meantime.
>
> [Sign-off from config.md]

## Rules

- **Ranges framing.** The email must call the numbers ballpark/estimates, never quote a locked figure. Consistency with the Gamma's "for discussion only" framing.
- **Both links.** Include the Gamma link and the booking link. The booking link is the conversion; don't drop it.
- **Fill from config.** Sign-off, scheduling link, and any phone/contact line come from `references/config.md`. If the scheduling link is still a placeholder there, tell Blaine in chat rather than sending a broken CTA.
- **One prospect per draft.** This is a personal reply, not a campaign. For a batch, that's a different tool (Levitate campaigns) — flag it, don't improvise a blast here.
