# Mundo Criatura

A creature-catching game for kids learning Spanish. Answer Spanish questions to
battle wild creatures, catch them, level them up, and watch them evolve. Every
creature's name is a real Spanish word.

Static site, no build step, no accounts. Works offline once loaded. Up to three
kids can share one device with separate players.

## How it plays

- Pick a zone, meet a wild creature, answer questions. Right answers hit it,
  wrong answers cost a heart and show the right word with a pronunciation hint.
- The device's built-in Spanish voice reads every word (slow by default).
- Partner creatures evolve at level 5 and level 10.
- Catch 3 creatures in a zone to unlock its boss and medal.

## Zones

1. Numbers 0 to 10
2. Numbers 11 to 20
3. Colors
4. Animals
5. Alphabet

A zone opens once about 70% of the previous zone's words are learned
(learned = right 2 times in a row).

## Parents

"Parents" and "Add or remove players" on the title screen sit behind a
multiplication question. The parent screen shows minutes played, words
learned, accuracy, and words that need practice. Progress is saved on the
device only.
